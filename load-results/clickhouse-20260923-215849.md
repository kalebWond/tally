# ClickHouse per-minute queries: 10,000,000 votes per contest

- **Data:** 10,000,000 synthetic votes for the contest under test over 3 h (926 votes/s average), 10,000,000 more for a second contest, 5% of the first contest's votes rejected. Loaded in 10.7 s.
- **Server:** ClickHouse 26.9.1.1629 in Docker on 11th Gen Intel(R) Core(TM) i5-1135G7 @ 2.40GHz, 8 threads, shared with the rest of the stack.
- **Timing:** server-side elapsed from `X-ClickHouse-Summary`, median of 5 runs after a warm-up, query cache off.

| Query | Time | Rows returned | Rows read | Data read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Votes per minute, whole contest: count() (duplicates counted) | **133.2 ms** | 180 | 10,000,000 | 228.9 MB |
| Votes per minute, whole contest: distinct keys (strings) | **567.7 ms** | 180 | 10,000,000 | 342.3 MB |
| Votes per minute, whole contest: distinct key hashes | **312.7 ms** | 180 | 10,005,862 | 305.4 MB |
| Counted per minute (accepted − rejected), key hashes | **330.1 ms** | 180 | 10,506,428 | 321.1 MB |
| Per minute per contestant (lead changes), key hashes | **392.8 ms** | 1,800 | 10,005,862 | 314.9 MB |
| By source, whole contest, key hashes | **408.3 ms** | 3 | 10,005,862 | 238.5 MB |
| Votes per minute, last 30 minutes, key hashes | **55.9 ms** | 30 | 1,679,360 | 51.1 MB |
